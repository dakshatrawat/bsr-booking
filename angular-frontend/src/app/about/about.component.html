About Bhagat Singh Resort

Your mountain sanctuary on the road to Badrinath

Welcome to the mountains

Bhagat Singh Resort sits in the quiet hills of Rudraprayag district, a comfortable stop for pilgrims and travellers heading towards Badrinath. Our rooms are designed for rest, with the mountain landscape right outside your window.

Whether you want a peaceful break, a base for exploring, or simply a place to reconnect with nature, we look after the essentials properly so you can enjoy the surroundings.
